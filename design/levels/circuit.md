# Circuit-Breaker Arena

Status: Phase 1C shipped in `levels/ffa_circuit.lvl` (generated by `scripts/gen_circuit_level.py`; 12 tests in
`crates/shinobi-sim/tests/arena_circuit.rs`, 9 in `level_features.rs`; 30 s headless smoke clean). The 8 solid piston
movers and the 4 `speed_lane` zones are live; client features are loaded/rendered by
`crates/shinobi-game/src/features_vis.rs` via `FeaturesRes` and predicted by `ClientGame::new_with_features`. The
`core_vent` zone is authored and remains a server trigger until the battery slice lands. Feature fingerprint
`0xfa02_e5e8_9d24_5248`. No `hazard` lines yet: glitch-trap and vent damage wait for the next slice.

## Identity

A symmetrical motherboard stadium built around a two-story CPU-fan nexus. Fast data lanes feed a contested core; the
map teaches wall-run chaining, piston parry timing and risk/reward vent control.

## Footprint

- 100 x 100 m stadium, floor deck at `y = 0`, void beyond the outer wall.
- Central nexus 30 x 30 m, spanning `y = -6` (sunken pit) to `y = 9` (upper ring).
- Four data lanes on the cardinals, each 10 m wide, 28 m long, walls 8 m tall.
- Outer spawn ring at `y = 0`, four piston pairs between the lanes and the nexus.
- No weapon pedestals; all weapons and mods are owned from spawn. Data cubes only for energy/health.

## Layout

### Data lanes

- North, east, south, west lanes, each with a 12 m run-up, a 20 m wall-run face and a 6 m exit.
- Wall-run face is 8 m tall so a climb cannot accidentally top out.
- Lane exit turns into the nexus instead of a dead end; each lane has a ground route and an upper hook anchor.
- One `speed_lane` zone per lane (1 m deep, 28 m long, y 0..8) hugs a single wall-run face, rotating with the lane.
  Tuning: `wall_run_m=10`, `mult=1.15`, `cap=1.6`, `decay=0.9`. They are live, not server-trigger-only: a wall-run
  inside a tagged lane gets the ramp/cap bonus and post-exit decay exactly as documented in `docs/movement.md`, and
  server and client prediction are bit-identical over 1000 ticks.

### Split-level nexus

- Lower pit: `y = -6`, 16 x 16 m, open to the vent, four ramps up to `y = 0`.
- Ground floor: 30 x 30 m ring around the pit, four entries aligned with the lanes.
- Upper ring: `y = 9`, 3 m wide wireframe platforms, four segments with 2 m gaps; grapple or wall-run access.
- Central fan column rises from the pit to `y = 14`; the upper ring braces onto it.
- Four 26.6° ramps at `z = ±5.0` and `±6.5` connect the pit floor to the deck. Pit walls are flush with the deck
  (`top y = 0`). The upper ring is four 12 x 3 m segments at `top y = 9` with 2 m corner gaps and four fan braces.
- No route is a single elevator: pit ramps, outer wall-runs and upper hooks all connect.

### Kinetic solenoid walls

- Eight solid `piston` movers in four pairs around `(±11, ±11)`, one pair per nexus diagonal.
- Each face is 1 m thick x 4 m tall x 2 m wide; the 6 m horizontal stroke runs at y 0..4.
- Cycle: `period_ticks=90` per leg, a 3 s full round trip; `phase_ticks=0/90` puts the paired tracks at opposite ends.
- Faces are solid server colliders and are predicted client-side, so they block or push a runner.
- Charge glow ramps over each leg and peaks at full extension; readable from every lane.
- Next slice: perfect parry launches along the face normal (target 12 m horizontal, 4 m up); a miss is a stagger and
  a damage event, not an instant kill. Glitch mines can latch to a face and ride the cycle.

### Core vent

- Blue plasma column in the lower pit, 8 m diameter, top at `y = -1`.
- One `core_vent` zone at `(0, -3.5, 0)` with half `(4, 2.5, 4)`; the fan column stands inside it.
- Tuning: `fill_ticks=90` (1.5 s), `cooldown_ticks=1200` (20 s), `reveal_ticks=300` (5 s).
- It remains a server trigger until the battery slice lands; filling the Battery, the HUD reveal and the cooldown
  dimming are the next slice, not live behaviour. Tuning is still `fill_ticks=90`, `cooldown_ticks=1200`,
  `reveal_ticks=300`.

## Spawns and pickups

- 10 spawns on the outer ring, two exits each, no direct sightline into the vent.
- 4 data cubes at lane mouths, 25 s respawn.
- 2 data cubes on the upper ring, 30 s respawn.
- All spawn and pickup boxes are permanent (`box`, not `dbox`).

## Art and readability

- PCB deck, copper traces, cyan lanes, white silkscreen.
- Piston face reads as a hammer; charge glow is visible at 60 m.
- Vent column is the strongest vertical silhouette in the map.
- Pit edge is marked by a hard emissive lip; no invisible falls.
