# 8v8 Tron team shooter: plan (owner: Engine / coordinator)

Vision: fast, cool, Tron-styled team arena shooter. Cyan vs Orange, 8 players per side (bots fill empty slots).
Movement (wall-run, dash, slide, grapple) is the identity; shurikens are the main weapon, katana is the close-range
risk/reward, block building is utility. Readability beats realism: you must always know who is friend, who is foe,
whether you hit, and who hit you.

## Status today
No team concept exists. Arena has 8 spawns and was tuned for 1v1..3v3. Everything below is new.

## Rules (v1: Team Deathmatch)
- Teams: id 0 = Cyan, 1 = Orange. Magenta stays the hazard / pickup colour. Auto-balance on join (smaller team, then
  fewest kills); late joiners fill the smaller side. Bots fill each side up to 8 (`--bots` / `--team-size 8`).
- Friendly fire OFF: shurikens fly through allies (no hit, no bounce), katana ignores allies, no self-knockback
  from allies. Enemy hits unchanged. Grapple/blocks may be used by allies.
- Score: team kills. Win at 50 team kills or 10 minutes, higher score wins, tie = sudden death 60 s. 8 s
  intermission, then team swap not required.
- Respawn 3 s at the TEAM's spawns (spawn-protection 1.5 s). Spawn choice keeps the "away from enemies" logic.
- v2 (after v1 is solid): capture/hold "grid nodes" (3 nodes, hold zone to score), then maybe a payload.

## Wire / state (Network owns)
- Team id per player: in WELCOME + scoreboard rows + a snapshot flag bit so any client knows any player's team.
- Team scores in match state / HUD events. Level file: `spawn x y z [team]` (0/1, omitted = either).
- Budget: 16 players + discs + ropes must fit one MTU snapshot per tick or degrade gracefully; keep the existing
  bandwidth test and extend it to 8v8 under load.

## Ownership
- Combat: team damage rules in pvp.rs / combat (friendly fire off, discs pass allies), team spawn selection in
  spawn.rs, team-aware bots in shinobi-ai (squad per team, no shooting allies). Finish sword feel + block building first.
- Network: team on the wire, auto-balance, team scores/events, `--team-size`, 16-player soak/perf test, level `spawn team`.
- HUD UI: team score bar (cyan vs orange), team-coloured scoreboard + killfeed, ally name tags/markers through walls
  (enemy: none or on hit), team-aware damage numbers; keep clear-periphery rules.
- Graphics: team colours on ninja bodies, discs, trails, hit sparks (ally vs enemy instantly readable), spawn-pad
  and base art, team-coloured grid; keep 16-player draw cost sane.
- Cam & Mov: author `levels/8v8.lvl` (see below) and tune movement on it; grapple anchor points; wall-run lanes.
- Engine (me): integration order, merge-health (shared branch must stay green), balance pass for 8v8 TTK, docs.

## Map brief: `levels/8v8.lvl` (Cam & Mov, with Graphics for look)
Symmetric (mirror across X or point-symmetric), ~1.5-2x the current arena. Two bases with 8 spawns each behind cover,
a contested centre with verticality, 2-3 long wall-run lanes, side flanks reachable by grapple, health/grid-beam
pickups on the flanks, no spawn with line of sight to the other base. Cover must also be shootable-around (shurikens
bounce, so corners matter).

## Balance targets (Engine, after teams land)
- TTK ~1.5-2 s body shots at 8v8 so focus fire matters, headshots ~1.1 s, katana 2 hits. Respawn 3 s.
- Aim assist stays small; revisit if team play feels too forgiving.
- Check per-tick server cost with 16 players + 100 discs (perf_step test) and keep under budget.

## Map v1 (Cam & Mov): `levels/8v8.lvl`
Generated by `scripts/gen_8v8_level.py` (edit the script, rerun, `cargo test -p shinobi-sim --test arena_8v8`). 112 x 112 m,
mirror-symmetric across z (Cyan base at -z, Orange at +z) and across x. Bases: 8 spawns each behind a 10 m front wall
(no sight line to the other base, tested), open sideways to the flanks. Three wall-run lanes (west/east flank lanes
32 m, central spine 24 m), two centre towers (tiers 1.2/2.4/3.6 + a 7.2 m pillar whose pickup is grapple-only), flank
shelves with pickups, grapple anchors: hanging slabs over the lane mouths and the flank gap, 14 m pylons.
Syntax: `spawn x y z team` (0 Cyan, 1 Orange), parsed by `parse_level_file` + `parse_spawn_teams`.
