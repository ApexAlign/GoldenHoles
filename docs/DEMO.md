# DEMO v0.1 plan: decisions, scope, checklist (owner delegated all design decisions to Engine until the demo)

Pitch: a Tron grid FFA arena shooter where you run on walls, grapple, lock on with a Predator triangle, throw ricocheting
discs, slice with a katana, and print floors/walls out of the grid that others can de-rez.

## Locked decisions (change only via Engine)
- Mode: FREE-FOR-ALL. Demo default: 1 human + 5 bots on the 88 m `levels/ffa_small.lvl` (`BOTS=5 ./demo.sh`). Up to 16 players. Match: first to 10 kills
  or 6 minutes, 2.5 s respawn (a death animation fills it: flash, collapse camera, the body bursts into fragments), 1.5 s spawn protection, 8 s intermission. Team mode stays hidden (`--teams` only).
- Default map (`./demo.sh`, `MAP=sky`): `levels/ffa_sky.lvl`, a floating vertical city, 232 x 190 m and 200 m tall, 248 boxes, 20 spawns, 10 pickups,
  no ground (kill plane y = -20). Four tiers of decks at y 24 / 55 / 86 / 117 (plates, catwalks, towers, canyons, terraces, arcades, chicanes)
  chained into one walkable serpentine by three long 24 degree ramps and turn plazas: every spawn walks to every deck with no hook and no jump.
  Around it: crown towers up to 200 m, five free-floating satellite towers and eight islands (disconnected: grapple or wall-run chains, 30 to 100 m
  gaps), 14 to 80 m wall-run faces everywhere. Nothing is a waist-high block (lips <= `Cfg::step_height`, ramps beside obstacles, or full walls: see
  `unwalkable_obstacles` and `tests/walkable.rs`). Generated by `scripts/gen_sky_level.py`. Bots walk all four tiers, keep off the edges (body-width
  edge guard) and do not hook on this map. `MAP=small` (88 m ground arena), `MAP=big`, `MAP=xl` pick the old maps.
- `MAP=isles` (**Skyfall Archipelago**, `levels/ffa_isles.lvl`, built by `scripts/gen_isles_level.py`): floating islands around a central ziggurat
  (HEAVEN'S GATE, y 60). Spokes lead to MOON GATE (east, a 34 m ring hung over the deck with a shrine disc in its eye and a bow on its crown),
  CASCADE (west, rice terraces by stairs and a 46 m waterfall wall), BAMBOO GROVE (north, 1 m stalks to wall-run, some leaning) and DRAGON BOWL
  (south, a sunken duel pit). A 66 m spire and a spiral of floating stones climb to the SKY SANCTUM (y 140, a bow), four corner shards and
  ~28 drifting rocks are hook / wall-run only. Bots walk every island. `MAP=isles ./demo.sh` (friends: same `MAP=` on `scripts/join.sh`).
- Map: `levels/ffa.lvl`, fixed (no shrinking, no void-start in the demo). Default `levels/ffa.lvl` = 224 m across (HALF=112). `levels/ffa_xl.lvl` = 336 m across (HALF=168) is optional; measured
  density favours the 224 m map (18.7 vs 8.7 kills/min with 16 players, Network). Layered: ground, mid towers, sky layer. Void edges + kill
  plane. 24+ spread spawn pads, no mutual sightlines.
- Building: N = floor/ramp (tap cycles), M = wall. Grid snapped. ANY player can destroy ANY piece, including their
  own. Piece hp 100; shuriken 12 dmg, katana 60 dmg to pieces; older pieces tougher (`age_bonus_per_sec` 0.02);
  size scales with owner kills `scale_per_kill` 0.1, `scale_cap` 2.0 (never more than 2x). Cap 64 pieces/player,
  build cooldown 12 ticks, range 6 m. Pieces de-rez into data fragments. No fall damage (kill plane only).
- Aim: Predator three-dot lock-on, acquire -> lock over 0.35 s, pull full only at lock, enemies only, needs LOS.
  Katana lunges toward the lock. Hit-stop 60 ms on katana hit (client-only).
- Combat (melee-first, `Balance::demo()`): 100 hp. Shuriken = the poke: 7 dmg, 5/s, ~2.8 s of clean fire to kill (headshot 1.5x).
  Katana = the weapon: 20 per cut, a cut every 0.42 s, the 3rd connected cut of a combo (1 s window) is a 30 hp finisher with +0.6 m
  reach, so 5 cuts (20/20/30/20/20) kill in ~1.7 s; the first cut carries the dash lunge (~5.7 m strike reach). The lock triangle
  reads SLASH when the target is inside cutting reach and flashes gold STRIKE while its strike window is open.
- Block / parry: hold Ctrl or F (right bumper on a pad). The view locks where it was when the shield went up, movement, jump, dash,
  grapple and wall-run all keep working, throwing and cutting are shut. Frontal hits are cut to 15 %, nothing from behind. A block raised
  within 0.25 s before a hit is a PARRY: the hit is negated and a melee attacker is staggered 0.8 s and open for 1 s. The guard lasts
  2.5 s held, each blocked hit costs 20 %, a spent guard re-arms after 1 s. Strike window: 0.25 s after your own cut and 1 s after
  being parried, hits on you are worth 1.5x (the HIT marker and `HitMsg.bonus` show it). Keys: `guard.*` Balance keys.
- Melee accuracy and heighteners (docs/melee.md): the katana lunge homes onto the lock, a target-centred cone forgives sloppy aim, cuts scale with your speed, launch their victims (wall-splat stun, void ring-outs credit the pusher), and katana kills stack a heal-and-damage streak.
- Grapple: E fire/release, pull + swing/orbit, speed cap 28 m/s, range 40 m.
- HUD: cyber-computer look, console/menus interactive, radar without wallhack.
- Audio: procedural synth behind the `audio` cargo feature, OFF by default; it may slip past the demo.
- Colours: unique neon per player (`player_colour`).

## Demo = scope freeze
IN: everything above. OUT (post-demo): teams polish, void-start map, capture/hold modes, ranked, new weapons.
After freeze only: bugs, polish of IN items, tests. No new systems without Engine approval.

## Demo-ready checklist (Engine runs it; each owner reports status)
1. `scripts/check.sh` green on golden-shinobi-main (workspace tests + game compile). 2. `BOTS=5 ./demo.sh` boots into the FFA
map, bots fight, a match ends, the next starts. 3. Controls work: WASD, jump, dash/roll/slide, wall-run, E grapple,
N/M build, LMB katana, RMB shuriken. 4. Lock-on triangle shows and assists. 5. Hit/kill/damage feedback on every
weapon, HUD panels live. 6. Grapple + build stay in sync at 5% loss (e2e tests). 7. Release soak at 16 players: p99
tick < 5 ms. 8. QUICKSTART has the demo commands; a tag `demo-v0.1` is pushed by Engine when 1-7 pass.
